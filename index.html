About

A lifelong instinct for melody, shaped for the screen.

Koen Janssen is a Belgian composer whose music has appeared in international campaigns and television productions for names including HBO, Universal, DC and NBC.

Raised in Hamme, Koen first discovered orchestral music as a child, conducting imaginary ensembles to Mozart and Beethoven before learning piano. His path later moved through electronic music, DJ sets, reggae, ska and band projects—experiences that now feed a cinematic sound balancing classical emotion with contemporary production.

Collaborations with artists, vocalists and live musicians have grown into a catalogue of albums and production music released through Warner Chappell Production Music, Elbroar Trailer Music and Color TV.
